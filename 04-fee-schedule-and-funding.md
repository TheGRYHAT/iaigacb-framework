# Fee Schedule & Funding — Who Pays What

IAiGACB · v0.1 · 2026-09-18 · **All figures are founding-chair proposals for board ratification.**

**Principle:** the regulated fund the regulator, in proportion to scale. Vendors pay to be governed and assessed. Operators pay for their own licenses. The Board never depends on one payer.

**Year one is voluntary and free.** Fees switch on at Phase 2 ([07-roadmap.md](07-roadmap.md)).

---

## 1. Vendor fees

### 1.1 Annual registration

Every AI company that commercially provisions a tiered system registers. The fee is **0.5% of annual AI-attributable revenue, with a floor and a cap.**

| AI revenue band | Annual fee |
|---|---|
| Under $2M | **$10,000** (floor) |
| $2M – $200M | **0.5% of AI revenue** |
| $200M – $1B | **$1,000,000** (cap reached at $200M) |
| Over $1B | **$1,000,000** + a **$250,000 Frontier Surcharge** per T3 system on the Register |

Revenue is self-reported under the Vendor Agreement with audit rights. "AI-attributable" means revenue from provisioning tiered systems — an enterprise software company with one AI feature isn't billed on its whole book.

### 1.2 Model assessment (per release)

Paid when a vendor submits a system for placement or re-assessment.

| Placement sought | Fee | What it funds |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | $15,000 | Document review, guardrail attestation check |
| T2 | $60,000 | Above + red-team report review + kill-switch verification |
| T3 | $250,000 | Above + independent Board evaluation (4–6 week engagement) |
| T4 flag | $150,000 per domain | Domain panel evaluation + Use Registration process setup |
| Re-baseline (annual, no capability change) | $5,000 | Register maintenance |

### 1.3 Integration & audit

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Registry API onboarding | $5,000 one-time |
| Quarterly provisioning audit (T4) | included in T4 flag fee |
| Annual provisioning audit (T3) | $25,000 |

### Organizational certification

| Level | Fee | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Org-L1 | $500 | Annual |
| Org-L2 | $2,500 | Annual |
| Org-L3 | $15,000 assessment + $3,000/yr | Assessment every 3 years |
| Org-L4 | Org-L3 + $10,000/yr per domain | Annual audit |

## 3. What the money is for

Year-two operating budget, assuming 12 registered vendors (2 over $1B), 40 model assessments, 5,000 L1, 800 L2, 60 L3, 10 L4, 200 organizations:

| Line | Revenue |
|---|---|
| Vendor registration | ~$3.1M |
| Model assessments | ~$2.4M |
| Operator exams + maintenance | ~$1.4M |
| Organizational certs | ~$0.8M |
| **Total** | **~$7.7M** |

| Line | Cost |
|---|---|
| Regulators / model assessors (6 FTE at credentialed-staff rates) | $1.8M |
| Registry engineering + hosting + security (3 FTE + infra) | $0.9M |
| Curriculum & exam operations (2 FTE + proctoring vendor) | $0.6M |
| Executive Director + operations (3 FTE) | $0.7M |
| Legal, insurance, audit, background-check vendor | $0.5M |
| Board stipends (year 2 on; see 05) | $0.3M |
| Reserve (target 6 months) | $1.5M |
| **Total** | **~$6.3M** |

The point of the vendor fee is the first line: **you cannot regulate a frontier lab with volunteers.** The half-percent buys the credentialed staff who actually read the red-team reports.

---

## 4. Independence rules

1. No single vendor's fees may exceed **25% of annual revenue**. If they would, the excess is escrowed to reserve and the Board recruits more vendors before spending it.
2. Vendor fees fund the Model Assessment Program; they do not fund exam development. Operator fees fund the exams. The Finance Committee reports the split annually.
3. Board members receive stipends, not salaries, and hold no vendor equity above a de-minimis threshold ([05-governance-and-board.md](05-governance-and-board.md)).
4. Fee changes require a two-thirds board vote and 90 days' public notice.

| Item | Cost | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Registry sandbox + public site | $40k | Founding sponsor (GRYHAT) — in-kind engineering |
| Legal formation (non-profit 501(c)(6) trade association) | $25k | Founding sponsor |
| Proctoring pilot (free L1/L2 exams, ~500 seats) | $15k | Founding sponsor |
| Board meetings, travel, insurance | $20k | Founding sponsor + member-institution hosting |
| **Total** | **~$100k** | |

That is the price of a standard with adopters before it has a price list.
